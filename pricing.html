Pricing & Services

Our service

We sell discounted digital coupons (codes or links) through our Telegram bot. All prices are in Indian Rupees (INR).

How prices work

  • Each coupon has its own price, which is shown in the bot before you pay. Prices can change from time to time.
  • The final amount, including any promo code discount you apply, is shown on the order summary before payment.
  • We do not charge a separate fee in the bot beyond the price shown on your order summary.

Payment methods

  • UPI: scan the QR in the bot, then submit your UTR for verification.
  • Wallet: pay from your in-bot wallet balance.

Wallet

  • Top-ups can be between ₹10 and ₹50,000 per top-up.
  • A top-up bonus may be offered from time to time; when active, it is shown in the bot.
  • Wallet balance is used to pay for coupons in the bot.

Order time limit

An order or wallet top-up that is not paid and verified within 30 minutes expires automatically. You can place a new one at any time.
