Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy explains what information Premium Coupon Bot (operated by GOVINDA KUMAR) handles when you use the Telegram bot and this website.

Information we collect in the bot

  • Your Telegram user ID and Telegram username (provided by Telegram when you message the bot).
  • When you first and last used the bot.
  • Your orders and wallet top-ups: items bought, quantity, amount, status and payment reference (UTR / order ID).
  • Your wallet balance, referral credits and referral relationships, if you use them.
  • Messages you send to the bot that are needed to process an order or support request.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your card number, bank login, UPI PIN or passwords. UPI payments are made in your own UPI app.

How we use it

  • To process orders, verify payments and deliver coupons.
  • To maintain your wallet balance and order history.
  • To provide support and resolve payment or delivery issues.
  • To send service messages and, from time to time, announcements or offers inside the bot.
  • To prevent misuse and fraud, including restricting accounts that abuse the service.

Payments

Payments are made via UPI or through a third-party payment gateway. The gateway handles your payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive only the result of the payment (for example amount, status and reference).

Storage and sharing

Bot data is stored with our hosting and database providers. We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers needed to run the service (such as hosting, database and payment providers), or where required by law.

Your choices

To ask about, correct or delete your data, write to realvidya01@gmail.com. We may need to keep some order records where required for accounting, fraud prevention or legal reasons.

Changes
